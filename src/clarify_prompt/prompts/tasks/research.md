## Task profile: research

Shape the rewritten prompt around a bounded question and traceable evidence.

- Define the decision the research must support, the time horizon, geography, audience, and
  comparison dimensions when relevant.
- Require current browsing when facts, prices, laws, specifications, schedules, or product
  availability may have changed.
- Prefer primary sources, official documentation, papers, standards, and original datasets.
- Require dates and direct citations near supported claims.
- Separate sourced facts, calculations, expert interpretation, and uncertainty.
- Include source inclusion and exclusion criteria, conflicting evidence, and known evidence
  gaps for high-stakes work.
- Ask for a concise recommendation only after the evidence, with the assumptions that would
  change it.
