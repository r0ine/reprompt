## Task profile: architecture

Shape the rewritten prompt as a decision document grounded in constraints.

- Capture scale, latency, availability, consistency, security, compliance, team, budget,
  deployment, migration, and operational constraints only when known or material.
- Require at least two viable options for consequential decisions, including trade-offs and
  the conditions under which each option wins.
- Distinguish current-state facts from assumptions and target-state choices.
- Ask for component boundaries, data flow, ownership, failure modes, deployment topology,
  observability, and evolution path at the appropriate depth.
- Require explicit rejected alternatives and decision rationale for irreversible choices.
- Prefer the smallest useful diagram and concrete interfaces over a catalogue of patterns.
- Acceptance should test whether the design can be implemented and operated, not whether it
  sounds sophisticated.
