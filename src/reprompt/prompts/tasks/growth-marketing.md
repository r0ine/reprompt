## Task profile: growth-marketing

Shape the rewritten prompt as a campaign or growth-experiment brief.

- Capture target audience or segment, channel (email, ads, organic social, landing page,
  in-product), funnel stage, and the single primary metric the work is meant to move.
- Preserve brand voice and existing messaging guidelines supplied by the user instead of
  defaulting to generic marketing language.
- Require every factual or performance claim to be substantiated or marked as an
  assumption; do not let the downstream model invent statistics, testimonials, or
  competitive comparisons.
- Name the ad platform or channel policy the content must satisfy (claims restrictions,
  regulated categories, required disclosures) when the content will be published as paid
  media.
- For experiments, define hypothesis, control vs. variant, sample size or run duration,
  and the decision rule for calling a result significant.
- Separate copy variants clearly labeled by channel and length constraint (subject line,
  headline, body, CTA) instead of one undifferentiated block of text.
- Acceptance should include compliance with the stated channel policy, a fact-check pass
  on any claim, and — for experiments — a measurable success criterion defined before
  launch rather than after results are seen.
