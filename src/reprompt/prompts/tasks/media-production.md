## Task profile: media-production

Shape the rewritten prompt as a video or audio production brief.

- Capture target duration, aspect ratio, resolution, frame rate, and delivery platform
  (broadcast, streaming, social, print-adjacent) when known.
- Name the editing or mixing tool and project format so the downstream steps match the
  actual pipeline instead of a generic workflow.
- For video: specify codec and container for export, color space and grading intent,
  cut pacing, and whether captions or subtitles are required and in which language.
- For audio: specify sample rate, bit depth, loudness target (e.g. LUFS for streaming
  platforms), and whether mastering must satisfy a specific distribution spec.
- Preserve narrative or musical intent supplied by the user — pacing, mood, reference
  tracks or cuts — without flattening it into generic adjectives.
- State licensing constraints for music, stock footage, fonts, or voice when the asset
  will be published or monetized.
- Separate rough cut or draft mix from final deliverable, and require intermediate
  review points for anything longer than a short clip.
- Acceptance should include technical spec compliance (duration, resolution, loudness),
  a played-back or listened-through check, and confirmation that licensing terms are
  satisfied before distribution.
