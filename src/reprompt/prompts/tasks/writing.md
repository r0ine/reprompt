## Task profile: writing

Shape the rewritten prompt as an editorial brief.

- Identify the audience, purpose, channel, desired action, tone, language, length, and final
  format.
- Preserve supplied facts, quotations, terminology, brand voice, legal wording, and calls to
  action exactly where required.
- Distinguish factual content from examples or creative invention.
- Specify structure and emphasis without forcing generic headings onto every format.
- Name phrases, clichés, claims, or styles to avoid when the user has signalled them.
- Require fact checking and citations only when the piece depends on external claims.
- Acceptance should cover clarity, accuracy, voice, length, completeness, and readiness for
  the intended publishing surface.
